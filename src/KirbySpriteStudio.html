Map

 
Edits save straight into the ROM copy (and are undoable). The map is recompressed on every stroke; if it no longer fits its original slot it is moved to free space at the end of the ROM automatically. Solid and enemy data are not editable yet, so tiles you paint change the look; whether they are solid depends on the game's tile rules.

Browse every sprite in your Kirby & the Amazing Mirror ROM and recolor it

Open your own ROM (or drop it on this page). Pick a sprite, pick an animation and frame, see the fully assembled sprite, then click any pixel to pick its color and change it.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your original file is never modified: you download a modded copy or an IPS patch.

Kirby colors: player colors & spray paints

The game has 16 Kirby color slots: the baseline pink Kirby, three friends, and the spray paints. Each slot points at a palette row. Pick a Body or Shoes color to recolor a slot with automatic shading, change which palette row a slot uses, or open the full editor. Edit slot 0 to change baseline Kirby; slots 1-3 are your friends.
